# Pitch outline

Seven slides at most, five minutes. Translated from the teammate's notes in `CLAUDE.md`; the slide order is his.
The deck must show team name, team member names and the challenge, plus images of the project, the user experience
and how NASA data and technology make it possible (`kb/challenge/event-and-judging.md`, section 2). Slides must be in
English. Cite requirement IDs from `kb/challenge/requirements.md` where a claim answers the challenge.

## Slides

1. **Problem.** Mission design is taught in an abstract way.
2. **Solution.** SK Horizon, with screenshots of the game.
3. **How it plays.** CML 1 to 4, launch, operation, report.
4. **Real trade-offs.** One example: the SAR radar (1.5 kW, 400 GB/day, needs a medium platform), or a CubeSat against a SmallSat.
5. **Team mode and educational value.** Four roles, shared approval, "why does it matter" explanations.
6. **Local impact.** Ecuador's volcanoes, El Nino, and NEE-01 Pegaso (Ecuador's first satellite, a 1U CubeSat, 2013).
7. **Technology, NASA resources, next steps and team.**
